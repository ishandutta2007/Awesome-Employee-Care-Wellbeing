## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce CAREforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/ten-years-of-careforce/)**  

  Salesforce's global health and safety program turning 10 in 2026. Built on the principle of equipping employees to care for one another through simple actions. Four calls to action: Communicate (report unsafe conditions), Assist (mutual support and Hardship Relief Fund), Respond (lifesaving action training), and Educate (continuous learning spanning mental wellbeing, workplace safety, and self-defense). In FY26 alone, over 700 trainings reached 27,000+ employees, with 97% of employees saying Salesforce is a physically safe place to work .



- **[Lyra Health](https://www.lyrahealth.com/)**  

  Leading workforce mental health benefits provider, available to 17+ million people globally. Features AI-powered provider matching, up to 12 annual therapy sessions, specialized care (LGBTQ+, couples, teen), and 24/7 clinical resources. Salesforce partners with Lyra for employee mental health, and 82.7% of people completing 8-12 sessions show clinical improvement .



- **[Modern Health](https://www.modernhealth.com/)**  

  Global, personalized mental health care platform. Combines coaching, therapy, and self-guided digital programs. Raised $130M Series D funding (Battery Ventures, Felicis Ventures). Designed for scalable, flexible care across global workforces .



- **[Spring Health](https://www.springhealth.com/)**  

  Personalized mental healthcare using assessment and machine-learning technology. Matches employees to care plans based on comprehensive condition assessment. Raised $190M+ in funding, focuses on prevention through AI-based risk assessments .



- **[Virgin Pulse (Personify Health)](https://www.virginpulse.com/)**  

  Enterprise-grade wellness platform covering physical health, mental wellbeing, financial wellness, sleep, nutrition, and preventive care. Integrates with wearables (Fitbit, Apple Health, Garmin) and health plans. Best suited for 500+ employee organizations with dedicated HR/benefits teams. $3-6/employee/month .



- **[Gympass (Wellhub)](https://www.gympass.com/)**  

  Corporate wellness platform providing fitness access to gyms, studios, and wellness apps globally. $5-25/employee/month. Best for fitness access across diverse locations .



- **[Calm for Business](https://www.calm.com/business)**  

  Sleep and meditation content for employees. Features Sleep Stories (celebrity-narrated bedtime narratives), meditation programs, breathing exercises, and Daily Calm. Calm Health offers clinical programs for anxiety, stress, and insomnia. $5-9/employee/year .



- **[Headspace for Work](https://www.headspace.com/work)**  

  Most recognized meditation brand. Features hundreds of guided meditations (3-30 min), workplace-specific sessions (pre-meeting calm, difficult conversations, focus sprints), sleep tools, and stress management. Companies report 30-40% monthly active usage. $6-12/employee/year .



- **[Wellable](https://www.wellable.co/)**  

  Wellness challenges and incentives platform. $75-100/month base + per-employee fees. Best for challenges and team engagement .



- **[Limeade](https://www.limeade.com/)**  

  Employee wellbeing solution focusing on holistic health. Named among top employee wellbeing market players alongside Virgin Pulse, Headspace, Wellable, and Gympass .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[MindBridge](https://github.com/guopanqi/mindbridge)**  

  Anonymous employee mental health support system for enterprises (license varies). Features anonymous identity within organizations, chat support, anonymous plaza, activities, appointments, and organizational reports. DingTalk and web invitation options. Three applications: employee H5 + Care Domain API, HR dashboard + healer case console, and DingTalk Stream private chat receiver. Built with Cloudflare Workers, D1 database, and AI-powered conversation harness .



- **[WorkMate](https://github.com/karlis0099/WorkMate)**  

  Employee wellbeing platform (license varies) — Java desktop application running silently in system tray with modern web UI at localhost:8765. Features weekly check-ins with 7 psychological wellbeing sliders (mood, energy, motivation, stress, work-life balance, team connection, accomplishment), real-time composite wellbeing score, burnout risk tracking, manager dashboard with heat-map colored survey data, and Slack/Teams-compatible burnout webhooks. SQLite persistence with SHA-256+salt authentication .



- **[Wellness Agent](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/wellness-agent)**  

  Enterprise-grade workplace wellness platform (TypeScript, license varies). Features 5 specialized AI agents, privacy-first multi-role support (employees, HR managers, employers), persistent memory system, real-time wellness tracking, and comprehensive organizational insights with role-based access .



- **[Sanicle AI](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/sanicle-ai)**  

  Comprehensive women's health platform (Next.js, license varies) designed for workplace wellness. Features personalized health tracking for female employees (menstrual health, menopause support) while providing HR departments with anonymized data insights for workforce planning. Inclusive work environment focus .



- **[Sanicle Cloud](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/sanicle-cloud)**  

  Women's health platform for workplace wellness (Next.js/React, license varies). Focuses on menstrual health management, menopause support, and inclusive work environments .



- **[Sanicle AI Mobile](https://github.com/ChanMeng666/sanicle-ai-mobile)**  

  Mobile-first FemTech web application (HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript, license varies). Features AI health assistant, period tracking, workplace integration, HR analytics, and comprehensive wellness monitoring optimized for mobile usage .



- **[WorkWell AI](https://github.com/topics/employee-wellbeing)**  

  End-to-end AI/ML system (license varies) predicting employee burnout and productivity risk using workload and behavioral data. Provides decision-support insights through REST API and interactive dashboard. Listed under GitHub employee-wellbeing topic .



- **[Health-Frontend](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@suman-jangili/health-frontend)**  

  Regulatory-first health check-in app for remote workers (React, MIT). Daily wellness questionnaire, manager dashboard with aggregated responses, policy viewer, HIPAA-compatible design respecting GDPR, CCPA, and DPDPA. Docker deployment with multi-stage build .



- **[WIA Mental-012 Workplace Wellbeing](https://github.com/WIA-Official/wia-standards-public)**  

  Employee mental health and wellbeing platform (MIT). Features evidence-based protocols, real-time analytics, HIPAA/GDPR compliance, multi-language support, and cross-platform compatibility .



- **[MindfulChat](https://github.com/topics/employee-wellbeing)**  

  Privacy-first employee wellbeing analytics frontend (TypeScript, license varies) with burnout detection, talent discovery, team health dashboards, and 3-agent AI chat. Listed under GitHub employee-wellbeing topic .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Bluecoats](https://github.com/topics/employee-wellbeing)** — Closed-loop, human-centric measurement and response program for health system staff (Jupyter Notebook). Diagnoses issues and develops custom solutions for wellbeing and operational improvement .

- **[Stress Detection for IT Professionals](https://github.com/suragms/STRESS-DETECTION-FOR-IT-PROFESSIONALS)** — AI-based stress detection using physiological signals and behavioral data. Real-time insights, personalized alerts, and chatbot support .

- **[WorkWell AI](https://github.com/topics/employee-wellbeing)** — AI/ML burnout prediction with REST API and dashboard .

- Community **n8n** workflows for wellness survey automation and Slack alerts.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **MindBridge** or **WorkMate** as the core wellbeing platform with **Wellness Agent** for AI-powered insights, **Health-Frontend** for regulatory-compliant check-ins, and **PostgreSQL/SQLite** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade employee care platform.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Employee wellbeing platforms handling health data must comply with data protection and privacy regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, etc.).

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, encryption at rest, and ongoing maintenance. Mental health data is sensitive and should be handled with appropriate care and clinical oversight.
